01 — The briefTwo ideas, one form

A consultancy where trust and protection cannot exist independently.

BETACH develops safety leadership and places specialist talent in the energy, industrial and high-hazard sectors of the Gulf. The identity had to carry operational discipline without losing the people at the centre of the work.

How can one identity communicate both human trust and structured protection?

Trust

The hand

Human connection, assurance and guidance — the relationships that carry consulting work.

Protection

The shield

Safety, resilience and responsibility — the discipline of high-hazard environments.

02 — ConstructionNegative space & geometry

Curves for warmth. Angles for discipline.

Four bars of equal width and equal spacing rest on a common line, their tips rising along a single diagonal. That line becomes the top edge of the shield. The palm closes the form, its edges held parallel, and a single curve gives the hand its human gesture.

The shield, held in the counter-space
Common line: fingertips meet the shield
Shared angle: fingertips, finger base and palm edge
Parallel base: thumb and palm
Single radius for the turn of the palm

05 — WordmarkUppercase, contemporary sans

Set in capitals for stability, with open counters that keep it calm rather than heavy.

The wordmark matches the symbol's straight verticals and firm horizontals, so the two read as one system.

Its weight is measured: confident, not aggressive. Professional, not bureaucratic.
